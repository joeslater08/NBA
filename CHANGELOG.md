# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 30 October 2026

- Added local FastAPI/React application and responsive research dashboard.
- Added relational storage and additive migrations for core records, historical logs, provenance and future model-run metadata.
- Added structured market definitions, manual grading, explicit void/postponement/overtime conventions and price-aware paper accounting.
- Added immutable prediction and settlement history, audit events, fixture/player validation and duplicate checks.
- Added analyst registry, singles statistics, linked parlays and virtual paper ledger.
- Added manual CSV provider, preview reports, atomic imports and CSV exports.
- Added fictional demonstration records, tests, local setup and backup utilities, integration contract and learning documentation.
- Forecasting, live ingestion and simulation remain planned; no real analyst history or NBA statistics are fabricated.
